50 A HISTORY OF EGYPT It was on some such occasion that one of these thieves secreted in a hole in the wall of the tomb the desiccated arm of Zer’s queen, still bearing under the close wrappings its splendid regalia (Fig. 17). Perhaps slain in some brawl, the robber, fortunately for us, never returned to recover his plunder, and it was found there and brought to Petrie intact by his well trained workmen in 1902.
